DAY 8 REPORT – SEGMENT PROFILING

OBJECTIVES
- Group customers into meaningful segments using CLV and churn risk.
- Profile each segment with average CLV, customer count, and churn rate.
- Add demographic profiling to understand differences across education and gender.
- Identify high‑value at‑risk customers for targeted retention strategies.

WORKFLOW
1. Defined CLV buckets (Low, Medium, High) using quantile segmentation.
2. Grouped customers by CLV segment and churn flag (Strict_Churn).
3. Calculated average CLV, customer count, and churn rate for each segment.
4. Extended profiling with demographic attributes (Gender, Education).
5. Compared segment patterns to highlight business priorities.

FUNCTIONS & CONCEPTS USED
- pd.qcut: quantile‑based segmentation of CLV.
- groupby + agg: aggregation of segment statistics.
- lambda with apply: churn rate calculation.
- Demographic profiling: multi‑attribute grouping.

RESULTS SUMMARY
CLV Segment Profiles
- Low CLV
  • Non‑Churn: Avg CLV ≈ 3,274 | Count = 117,497 | Churn Rate = 89.7%
  • Churn: Avg CLV ≈ 3,312 | Count = 13,550 | Churn Rate = 10.3%
- Medium CLV
  • Non‑Churn: Avg CLV ≈ 6,076 | Count = 116,402 | Churn Rate = 88.9%
  • Churn: Avg CLV ≈ 6,053 | Count = 14,595 | Churn Rate = 11.1%
- High CLV
  • Non‑Churn: Avg CLV ≈ 14,548 | Count = 117,043 | Churn Rate = 89.4%
  • Churn: Avg CLV ≈ 15,274 | Count = 13,849 | Churn Rate = 10.6%

Demographic Profile (Low CLV, Female)
- Bachelor: Avg CLV ≈ 3,260 | Churn ≈ 11.0%
- College: Avg CLV ≈ 3,286 | Churn ≈ 10.4%
- Doctor: Avg CLV ≈ 3,440 | Churn ≈ 14.4%
- High School or Below: Avg CLV ≈ 3,270 | Churn ≈ 10.1%
- Master: Avg CLV ≈ 3,308 | Churn ≈ 6.6%

INTERPRETATION
- Across all CLV segments, churn rates hover around 10–11%, showing consistent risk distribution.
- High CLV churners (Avg CLV ≈ 15,274) represent the most critical segment: fewer in number but high revenue loss potential.
- Medium CLV churners are slightly larger in count, making them important for volume‑based retention.
- Demographic profiling reveals variation: e.g., Low CLV females with Master’s education have lower churn (~6.6%), while Doctorates show higher churn (~14.4%).
- These insights suggest targeted strategies: prioritize retention for high‑CLV churners and investigate demographic drivers of churn.
