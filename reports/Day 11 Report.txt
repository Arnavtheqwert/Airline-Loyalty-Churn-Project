DAY 11 REPORT – RECOMMENDATION ENGINE PROTOTYPE

OBJECTIVES
- Build a prototype system that automatically suggests retention actions for each customer.
- Use CLV segment, churn risk, and geography to generate tailored recommendations.
- Prepare the framework for integration into the Day 12 dashboard.

WORKFLOW
1. Defined CLV segments (Low, Medium, High) using quantile segmentation.
2. Applied rules combining CLV segment, churn flag, and geography:
   • High CLV churners → concierge outreach + tier upgrade.
   • Medium CLV churners → bundle offers + loyalty progression.
   • Low CLV churners → discounts + gamification.
   • Non‑churners → maintain engagement.
   • Geographic modifiers (British Columbia, Calgary) → localized perks.
3. Implemented rule‑based function to assign recommendations.
4. Previewed recommendation assignments for sample customers.

FUNCTIONS & CONCEPTS USED
- pd.qcut: CLV segmentation.
- apply with custom function: rule‑based recommendation assignment.
- Rule‑based engine design: mapping business logic to customer attributes.

RESULTS SUMMARY (sample preview)
- Loyalty Number 100590 | CLV Segment: High | Strict_Churn: 0 | City: Vancouver → Recommendation: Non‑churner → Maintain engagement
- High CLV non‑churners are correctly assigned “Maintain engagement.”
- Churners in each segment would receive differentiated strategies (concierge outreach, bundle offers, discounts).

INTERPRETATION
- The prototype demonstrates how customer attributes can be mapped to actionable retention strategies.
- High CLV churners in priority regions (e.g., British Columbia, Calgary) will receive premium localized interventions.
- Medium and Low CLV churners are targeted with scalable, cost‑effective tactics.
- Non‑churners are maintained with standard engagement to preserve loyalty without overspending.
- This rule‑based engine provides a foundation for automation and will be integrated into the dashboard in Day 12.

DELIVERABLES
