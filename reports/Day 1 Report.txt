Day 1 Report – Data Preparation & Cleaning

1. Objective
The goal of Day 1 was to prepare and clean the Customer Flight Activity and Customer Loyalty History datasets to ensure consistency, remove anomalies, and create a reliable foundation for churn modeling and segmentation.

2. Data Sources
- Customer Flight Activity.csv → Records of flights, points accumulated/redeemed, distances, and costs.
- Customer Loyalty History.csv → Customer demographics, enrollment/cancellation details, loyalty tier, and CLV.
- Merge Key: Loyalty Number

3. Cleaning Workflow
Step 1: Load & Merge
- Used pd.read_csv() to load both files.
- Merged on Loyalty Number with inner join to ensure only customers with both flight activity and loyalty profiles were included for anomaly checks.

Step 2: Anomaly Detection & Fixes
1. Redemption > Accumulation
   - Rule: Redeemed points cannot exceed accumulated points.
   - Fix: Capped Points Redeemed at Points Accumulated.

2. Cancelled but Still Active
   - Rule: No flights after cancellation.
   - Fix: Reset cancellation dates to None if activity continued.

3. Zero Flights but Redeemed Points
   - Rule: Redemption without flights is unusual.
   - Fix: Added flag Redeem_Without_Flights_Flag (1 = anomaly).

4. Invalid Distances (≤0 km)
   - Rule: Flights must have positive distance.
   - Fix: Added flag Invalid_Distance_Flag only when Total Flights > 0.

5. Enrollment vs Cancellation Mismatch
   - Rule: Cancellation cannot occur before enrollment.
   - Fix: Reset cancellation dates to None if mismatch detected.

6. Missing Demographics
   - Rule: Salary, Education, Gender, Marital Status should be complete.
   - Fix:
     - Imputed Salary with median by education group.
     - Filled missing Education with mode.
     - Added flag Missing_Demo_Flag for remaining gaps.

Step 3: Save Cleaned Dataset
- Exported cleaned dataset as cleaned_loyalty_data.csv using to_csv().
- Preserved original row‑wise format with added flags.

4. Churn Definition (Foundation for Day 2)
Two churn definitions were established for future modeling:
- Strict churn: A customer is churned if they have a recorded cancellation date.
- Behavioral churn: A customer is churned if they show inactivity (no flights, no accumulation, no redemption) for a defined period, even without cancellation.

This dual definition ensures both official cancellations and silent drop‑offs are captured.

5. Business Rationale for Anomalies
- Redeem > Accumulate: Indicates fraud, promotional misuse, or data entry error. Distorts CLV and loyalty economics.
- Cancelled but Active: Suggests incorrect cancellation records or system lag. Mislabels active customers as churned.
- Zero Flights but Redeemed Points: May reflect partner redemptions (non‑flight offers). Important to separate from flight‑based loyalty.
- Invalid Distances: Impossible values undermine travel metrics and segment analysis.
- Enrollment vs Cancellation Mismatch: Logical inconsistency that breaks churn timelines.
- Missing Demographics: Reduces segmentation accuracy. Imputation ensures models remain robust while flags preserve transparency.

6. Missing Demographics (Detailed Subsection)
Demographic fields (Salary, Education, Gender, Marital Status) are essential for segmentation and churn modeling. Missing values were handled as follows:
- Salary Imputation: Filled missing salaries with the median salary of customers in the same education group.
- Education Imputation: Filled missing education with the most common (mode) education level.
- Flagging Remaining Gaps: Added Missing_Demo_Flag column to mark rows where any demographic field remained missing after imputation.

Example:
Loyalty Number | Salary | Education | Gender | Marital Status | Missing_Demo_Flag
201 | 50000 | Graduate | Male | Married | 0
202 | NaN → 45000 (median for Graduate) | Graduate | Female | Single | 0
203 | 60000 | NaN → Graduate (mode) | Male | Married | 0
204 | NaN | NaN | NaN | NaN | 1

Rationale:
- Imputation ensures the dataset remains usable for modeling and segmentation.
- Flagging preserves transparency, allowing analysts to treat incomplete records cautiously.
- This balances data quality with business practicality.

7. Functions & Concepts Learned
- Data loading: pd.read_csv()
- Merging: pd.merge() with how="inner"
- Conditional updates: .loc[], .notna()
- Flagging anomalies: .astype(int)
- Group imputation: .groupby().transform(), .median()
- Mode filling: .mode(), .fillna()
- Row checks: .any(axis=1)
- Export: .to_csv()
