DAY 6 REPORT – ENSEMBLE METHODS

OBJECTIVES
- Explore ensemble techniques (Voting and Stacking) to combine Random Forest and XGBoost.
- Evaluate whether ensembles improve performance compared to individual models.
- Document findings and interpret business implications.

WORKFLOW
1. Recreated tuned Random Forest and XGBoost models from Day 4.
2. Built a Voting Classifier (soft voting) combining Random Forest + XGBoost.
3. Built a Stacking Classifier with Random Forest and XGBoost as base learners, Logistic Regression as meta‑learner.
4. Evaluated ensemble performance using Accuracy, Precision, Recall, F1 Score, and ROC AUC.

FUNCTIONS & CONCEPTS USED
- VotingClassifier: combines predictions via majority or probability averaging.
- StackingClassifier: meta‑learner approach combining base model outputs.
- LogisticRegression: used as meta‑learner in stacking.
- sklearn.metrics: evaluation metrics.

Stacking Classifier

INTERPRETATION
- Voting Classifier failed to predict churners (precision/recall = 0), essentially defaulting to majority class.
- Stacking Classifier showed modest improvement: recall ~23%, precision ~58%, F1 ~0.33, ROC AUC ~0.61.
- Neither ensemble outperformed the tuned Random Forest (Day 4–5 results: F1 ~0.98, ROC AUC ~0.98).
- Random Forest remains the most reliable production model, while ensembles highlight the difficulty of improving recall without sacrificing precision.
