DAY 2 REPORT – FEATURE ENGINEERING & CHURN FLAG CREATION

OBJECTIVES
- Enhance the cleaned dataset from Day 1 with new behavioral and demographic features.
- Define churn in multiple ways (strict vs. behavioral) to capture different disengagement patterns.
- Prepare a robust dataset for modeling in Day 3.

WORKFLOW
1. Data Preparation
   - Loaded cleaned_loyalty_data.csv (Day 1 output).
   - Preserved anomaly flags from Day 1 (Redeem_Without_Flights_Flag, Invalid_Distance_Flag, Missing_Demo_Flag).

2. Feature Engineering
   - Flight_Frequency: Total Flights ÷ (2026 – Enrollment Year).
   - Redemption_Ratio: Points Redeemed ÷ Points Accumulated (with zero-handling).
   - Season: Enrollment Month mapped to Winter, Spring, Summer, Fall.
   - Tier_Code: Loyalty Card mapped to numeric codes (Blue=1, Silver=2, Gold=3, Platinum=4).
   - CLV_Segment: Binary segmentation based on median CLV.

3. Churn Flag Creation
   - Strict_Churn: Cancellation Year present → churned.
   - Behavioral_Churn: No flights, no accumulation, no redemption → inactive.

4. Dataset Output
   - Saved engineered_loyalty_data.csv with all Day 1 flags + new features + churn flags.

FUNCTIONS & CONCEPTS USED
- pandas: data loading, merging, feature creation.
- pd.cut: binning enrollment months into seasons.
- map: converting loyalty card tiers into numeric codes.
- median: CLV segmentation.
- Boolean logic: churn flag definitions.

RESULTS SUMMARY
- Engineered features created: Flight_Frequency, Redemption_Ratio, Season, Tier_Code, CLV_Segment.
- Churn flags defined: Strict_Churn, Behavioral_Churn.
- Final dataset: engineered_loyalty_data.csv with ~16,700 records, anomalies preserved, features added.

INTERPRETATION
- Flight_Frequency and Redemption_Ratio capture behavioral intensity.
- Season and Tier_Code add demographic and program-level context.
- CLV_Segment provides a value-based split for retention analysis.
- Strict vs. Behavioral churn definitions ensure both formal cancellations and silent disengagement are captured.

DELIVERABLES
- Final engineered dataset (engineered_loyalty_data.csv).
- This Day 2 Report summarizing objectives, workflow, functions, results, and insights.
