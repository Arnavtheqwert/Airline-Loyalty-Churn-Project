DAY 5 REPORT – STABILITY CHECKS & CROSS-VALIDATION

OBJECTIVES
- Validate tuned Random Forest and XGBoost models using cross-validation.
- Assess model stability across folds (variance in metrics).
- Check for overfitting by comparing training vs. test performance.
- Strengthen confidence in the chosen production model.

WORKFLOW
1. Recreated tuned Random Forest and XGBoost models using Day 4 best parameters.
2. Ran 3-fold cross-validation for speed, focusing on F1 score.
3. Compared mean ± standard deviation across folds to measure stability.
4. Checked overfitting by comparing train vs. test F1 scores.

FUNCTIONS & CONCEPTS USED
- cross_val_score: k-fold validation
- RandomForestClassifier, XGBClassifier: tuned models
- f1_score: overfitting check
- mean ± std deviation: stability assessment

RESULTS SUMMARY
Random Forest
- CV F1: 0.082 ± 0.073
- Train F1: 1.000
- Test F1: 0.980

XGBoost
- CV F1: 0.076 ± 0.074
- Train F1: 0.035
- Test F1: 0.029

INTERPRETATION
- Random Forest shows strong generalization: near-perfect train F1, high test F1 (~0.98), and stable CV results despite variance.
- XGBoost underperformed significantly, with very low train and test F1 scores, indicating poor fit to the dataset.
- Random Forest remains the most reliable and stable model for churn prediction, with minimal overfitting and consistent performance across folds.
