DAY 4 REPORT – HYPERPARAMETER TUNING & FEATURE IMPORTANCE

OBJECTIVES
- Optimize Random Forest and XGBoost models using hyperparameter tuning.
- Compare tuned models against Day 3 baseline results.
- Identify the most important features driving churn predictions.
- Strengthen the credibility and interpretability of the modeling pipeline.

WORKFLOW
1. Hyperparameter Tuning
   - Random Forest tuned with GridSearchCV across n_estimators, max_depth, min_samples_split, min_samples_leaf.
   - XGBoost tuned with RandomizedSearchCV across n_estimators, learning_rate, max_depth, subsample, colsample_bytree.
   - Selected best models based on F1 score.

2. Model Evaluation
   - Computed Accuracy, Precision, Recall, F1 Score, ROC AUC.
   - Compared tuned results with Day 3 baseline.

3. Feature Importance
   - Extracted top 10 features from Random Forest and XGBoost.
   - Interpreted business meaning of key drivers.

FUNCTIONS & CONCEPTS USED
- GridSearchCV, RandomizedSearchCV: hyperparameter tuning
- RandomForestClassifier, XGBClassifier: tuned models
- feature_importances_: feature ranking
- sklearn.metrics: evaluation metrics

INTERPRETATION
- Random Forest improved slightly over Day 3 baseline, achieving excellent balance between Precision and Recall.
- Loyalty Number, CLV, and Salary are the strongest churn predictors in Random Forest, highlighting customer identity, value, and demographics.
- XGBoost tuning did not improve recall; it remains weak at ~28%, indicating difficulty in capturing churners.
- Flight_Frequency and Total Flights dominate XGBoost importance, showing behavioral activity as its main driver.
- Best Model: Random Forest remains the most reliable and interpretable choice for churn prediction.
