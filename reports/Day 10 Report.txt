DAY 10 REPORT – RETENTION STRATEGY DESIGN

OBJECTIVES
- Translate churn and segment insights into actionable retention strategies.
- Assign strategies to customers based on CLV segment and churn risk.
- Document a framework for personalized engagement and campaign testing.

WORKFLOW
1. Defined CLV segments (Low, Medium, High) using quantile segmentation.
2. Mapped churners in each segment to tailored retention strategies:
   • High CLV churners → concierge outreach + tier upgrade.
   • Medium CLV churners → bundle offers + loyalty progression.
   • Low CLV churners → discounted fares + gamification.
   • Non‑churners → maintain engagement.
3. Applied strategy mapping function across dataset.
4. Previewed assignments to confirm logic.

FUNCTIONS & CONCEPTS USED
- pd.qcut: CLV segmentation into quantiles.
- apply with custom function: strategy assignment per customer.
- Retention strategy design: linking business actions to churn insights.

RESULTS SUMMARY (sample preview)
- Loyalty Number 100590 | CLV Segment: High | Strict_Churn: 0 → Strategy: Maintain engagement
- High CLV non‑churners are assigned “Maintain engagement” to preserve loyalty.
- Churners in each segment receive differentiated strategies based on value.

INTERPRETATION
- Framework ensures high‑value churners receive premium interventions (tier upgrades, concierge outreach).
- Medium CLV churners are targeted with bundle offers and progression incentives to increase loyalty.
- Low CLV churners are engaged with cost‑effective tactics (discounts, gamification).
- Non‑churners are maintained with standard engagement to avoid unnecessary cost.
- This segmentation‑driven approach balances retention investment with customer value.
